Data science for a better world

The Master of Population Data Science is a STEM-designated program that builds data science and AI skills and applies them to questions that matter. You’ll learn from world-renowned social and data scientists at the nation’s #1 public university, in one of the most diverse and vibrant cities in the world.

  1. 01

    What is population data science?

    Population data science uses data to understand how people live, work, partner, and move and how those choices shape a changing world. It combines theory from demography, economics, geography, political science, public health, and sociology with modern statistics to answer questions that matter for policy, business, and people’s lives.

    Meet the faculty
  2. 02

    Why MPDS?

    AI can write code and process data in seconds. The hard part is knowing which questions are worth asking and how well the data answers them. You’ll learn from world-renowned faculty to distinguish correlation from causation, to find gaps in the evidence, and to turn results into decisions with impact. MPDS prepares you to use data science and AI to identify and solve real problems in business, technology, public policy, health, government, nonprofits, and research. MPDS also prepares students who plan to pursue PhDs.

    See what you’ll study
  3. 03

    Who should apply?

    Do you have a background in the social sciences, public health, or policy and want to build technical skills in data science? Do you have a degree in data science or statistics but are interested in social science questions? Or, are you curious but coming from another field? Our flexible curriculum is designed to challenge you, both broadening and deepening your skill set, and prepare you to work with data and AI in some of the fastest-growing sectors of the economy.
