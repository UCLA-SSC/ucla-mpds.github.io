What you’ll study

  1. 01

    Social Science

    How to ask the right questions
    4 units (1 course)

    Learn the methods social scientists use to model human decisions and population change, drawing on demography, economics, geography, political science, public health, and sociology. You’ll learn to frame questions data can answer and to measure what matters.

  2. 02

    Data Science

    Causal inference and AI tools
    8 units (2 courses)

    Learn descriptive methods and quasi-experimental methods that separate cause from correlation. Build replicable workflows and use AI tools to extend your analysis in hands-on assignments. You’ll learn to interpret output and judge when a claim holds up.

  3. 03

    Professional Skills

    Coding, collaboration, and communication
    8 units (2 courses)

    Build hands-on skills in programming, machine learning, and AI-assisted coding; managing large-scale datasets; and visualizing statistical results. Practice team collaboration, project management, and professional presentations to non-technical audiences.

  4. 04

    Data Science in Action

    Career seminar · 10 units

    Each week, internationally renowned speakers from academia, industry, government, and nonprofits help broaden your understanding of how to deploy data science to solve real problems. Meetings with speakers provide career-building tips and networking opportunities.

  5. 05

    Mentored Capstone Project

    Apply your skills · 6 units (9 months)

    Over nine months, tackle a real-world question from research or industry. Starting from a problem statement, you’ll build and carry out a work plan with a small team of peers and a faculty mentor, write briefs for stakeholders, and finish with an original analysis and presentation.

  6. 06

    Choose from 40+ UCLA Electives

    Electives · 12 units (3 courses)

    Take courses by leading faculty across campus to deepen your expertise and broaden your horizons, including courses in demography, economics, geography, political science, public health, sociology, and statistics and data science.

Program FAQs

  • Are classes in person?

    Yes. All MPDS courses are taught in person on the UCLA campus. The program does not offer online or hybrid courses.

  • Can I finish the program in 16 months?

    Yes. With the program’s approval and an approved capstone project, you can take 10 to 12 units a quarter and graduate in 16 months instead of 20. Students on the accelerated track complete the capstone in a team with other accelerated students.

  • Is an internship required?

    No. A summer internship between the first and second years is optional. Program advisors help you arrange one with a research center, government agency, nonprofit, or company.

  • Is MPDS a STEM-designated program?

    Yes. MPDS is designated a STEM program. This means that international graduates may be eligible for up to 36 months of Optional Practical Training (OPT). See the federal STEM OPT guidance.
